About Me

I am a computer scientist working on machine learning, LLM agents, and the reliability of data-driven systems. I am the founding Scientific Director of Instituto Curvelo, a non-profit R&D institute, and a postdoctoral researcher at the Federal University of São Paulo, where I develop Mensura: a statically typed language for data handling that turns data leakage into a compile-time error, with its core algebra formally verified in Lean 4. I teach and advise graduate students at ITA and Unifesp, and I wrote a book on data science projects focused on validation and data leakage.

Above all, I am a Christian. My faith in Christ comes first and shapes how I live, work, teach, and lead. I am a happy husband and a proud father of two. In my free time: programming, science fiction (books and movies), and lots of coffee ☕.

Projects and Resources

  • Mensura: a typed language for data handling in which data leakage is a compile-time error, built on a split-invariant algebra formally verified in Lean 4.
  • Instituto Curvelo: applied R&D in generative AI (RAG, MCP), multi-agent LLM systems, computer vision, embedded systems and IoT, and industrial automation.
  • Wildfire risk prediction: drone, satellite, and ground-sensor data for spatio-temporal risk models, with the Brazilian Air Force (IEEE Access, 2024).
  • GNSS-free aerial navigation: image-based navigation with multi-sensor fusion, with the Brazilian Air Force, IEAv, and Embraer (Sensors, 2024).
  • DroneComp: computer systems and computational methods for the next generation air transport system.
  • Books: Projeto de Ciência de Dados (LTC/GEN, 2026, in Portuguese) and Data Science Project: An Inductive Learning Approach (free on Leanpub).
